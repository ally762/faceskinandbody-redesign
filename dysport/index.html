Why Everyone Says "Botox"

In the world of aesthetics, one name has become shorthand for an entire category of neuromodulators: BOTOX™. It's a phenomenon similar to calling all facial tissues Kleenex™ or all adhesive bandages Band-Aids™. The brand name has simply become the common noun people use to describe any product in that class.

In reality, BOTOX™ is just one of more than four different neuromodulator medications officially approved by Health Canada. Each of these prescription medications works to temporarily relax muscles and smooth wrinkles - but they all have subtle differences in molecular structure, onset time, and diffusion rates.

During your initial visit, our Registered Nurse Injector performs a detailed assessment of your medical history and specific goals. After evaluating the lines and wrinkles in the areas you wish to treat, they will then, with the guidance of our Medical Director, prescribe the exact type of neuromodulator medication that will work best for your skin and facial anatomy.

How Dysport Works

Dysport™ is an injectable prescription medicine that temporarily relaxes the specific facial muscles responsible for repeated expressions. When we laugh, frown, or squint, the muscle movement creates dynamic wrinkles and lines. This treatment interrupts the signal between the nerve and the muscle, causing the muscle to relax. By allowing the muscle to relax, Dysport™ effectively softens the look of existing deep wrinkles and prevents future lines from developing. The entire Dysport™ procedure is quick and delivers a smoother, refreshed, natural-looking result.

Benefits Of Dysport

Dysport™ provides numerous benefits:

  • Smooths Wrinkles and Lines: It significantly reduces the appearance of moderate to severe lines and wrinkles, including crow's feet and forehead wrinkles.
  • Natural Results: Our precise Dysport™ treatment ensures movement is preserved where desired, giving you beautifully natural results. We aim to enhance your natural beauty.
  • Quick Treatment: The treatment takes as little as 15 to 25 minutes, fitting easily into your busy schedule.
  • Preventative Care: Regular Dysport™ injections prevent dynamic lines from etching into static wrinkles.
  • Versatility: Dysport™ can also be paired with lip fillers and other procedures for total facial rejuvenation. Understanding BOTOX™ and Dysport™ options allows us to tailor a plan just for you.

Ideal Candidates

The ideal candidate is seeking to treat dynamic wrinkles or fine lines. If you have visible lines when your face is at rest, or deep lines when you animate, Dysport™ can help.

Dysport™ is a powerful solution for concerns such as:

  • Frown lines between the eyebrows (glabella)
  • Horizontal forehead wrinkles
  • Crow's feet around the eyes
  • Subtle fine lines around the mouth

We frequently use Dysport™ as a treatment in conjunction with lip or cheek fillers for a balanced approach to natural beauty. We will thoroughly assess whether Dysport™ is the right neuromodulator treatment for your goals during your consultation.

What To Expect From Treatment

Your Dysport™ treatment begins with a thorough consultation to assess your lines and discuss your desired natural results. The treatment itself involves a series of quick, fine injections into the target muscle groups.

Number Of Treatments Required

The effects of one treatment of Dysport™ typically last between three and four months. To maintain beautifully smooth skin, we recommend scheduling Dysport™ treatments 3 to 4 times per year. Consistency is key to preventing deep wrinkles and fine lines.

Results And Recovery

Following your Dysport™ procedure, there is virtually no downtime. You may experience slight redness or minor swelling at the treatment sites, which typically subsides within hours.

While some patients notice a softening of lines within a few days, the final, full natural results of your Dysport™ treatment will be visible after about two weeks. We recommend scheduling a follow-up appointment around two weeks after your initial neuromodulator treatment to review your results and ensure you are completely satisfied. The relief from deep wrinkles is worth the wait!
