What Is A Men’s Facial?

This type of facial is designed specifically for men to address the unique needs of male skin, which is often thicker, oilier, and more prone to razor burn and ingrown hairs. Our personalized men's facial at Face Skin & Body Bar in Calgary goes beyond a basic cleanse. It's a comprehensive grooming experience that incorporates deep cleansing to remove impurities and excess oil. This specialized facial includes a relaxing massage to ease tension in the face, neck, and shoulders, promoting circulation and a refreshed feeling. It also focuses on exfoliation to slough away dead skin cells, improving skin tone, texture & brightening. We tailor each facial to your individual concerns, ensuring optimal results.

Benefits Of Facial Treatments For Men

A regular facial offers numerous benefits for men. The face massage component helps to relieve stress and improve blood flow, contributing to healthier skin. Our deep cleansing helps to prevent breakouts and ingrown hairs caused by shaving. This treatment can significantly improve tone, texture & brightening, leaving you looking and feeling refreshed. Furthermore, the targeted techniques and products used in our men's facial can address specific concerns like dryness, sensitivity, and the early signs of aging. Regular massage can also promote lymphatic drainage, reducing puffiness.

What To Expect From Treatment

Here's a step-by-step guide to your experience:

  1. Personalized Skin Analysis: Your treatment begins with a thorough consultation and skin analysis to understand your concerns and skin type, ensuring the facial is tailored just for you.
  2. Deep Pore Cleansing: A powerful, yet gentle cleanser will remove surface impurities, excess oil, and grime, setting the stage for deeper purification.
  3. Exfoliation & Prep: A customized exfoliant will be applied to slough off dead skin cells, helping to smooth rough texture and prepare your skin for extractions. Warm steam will further open pores.
  4. Targeted Extractions: If needed, our Medical Aestheticians will meticulously perform extractions to clear congested pores, blackheads, and address any ingrown hairs with precision.
  5. Soothing Mask: A calming and balancing mask, chosen for your skin's needs, will be applied to reduce redness, soothe irritation (especially post-shave), and refine pores.
  6. Relaxing Face & Arm Massage: Enjoy a revitalizing massage of your face, shoulders, arms, and hands, designed to ease tension and promote relaxation.
  7. Hydration & Protection: The facial concludes with the application of high-quality serums, a targeted moisturizer, and a protective SPF, tailored to hydrate and safeguard your skin.
  8. Expert Advice: Receive personalized recommendations for your at-home skincare routine to maintain your results.

Number Of Treatments Required

The number of facial treatments required will vary depending on your individual skin concerns and goals. For maintenance and general skin health, a monthly facial can be highly beneficial. To address specific issues like acne or significant dryness, a series of treatments may be recommended, followed by maintenance sessions. Our estheticians will assess your skin and recommend a personalized treatment plan.
