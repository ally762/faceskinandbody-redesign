The Add Ons

Add on any of the following treatments to your targeted facial to get the customized results you need.

Lip Treatment

$25

This add-on is a restorative and effective remedy for dry, dehydrated lips. The treatment includes a lip scrub, lip mask and hydrating lip serum to leave skin feeling nourished.

Decollette Treatment

$40

This add-on is designed to target an area of the body that is often forgotten about: the neck & upper chest. As an extension of your facial, this service will apply the same techniques and products to your neck and decollete, which will keep your neck looking young and rejuvenated.

Eye Treatment

$25

This add-on is a soothing, gentle and hydrating treatment for tired eyes. A combination of targeted eye masks, hydrating serums and a specially designed micro-current device help reduce fine lines and puffiness.

Dermaplaning

$55

This add-on is a non-invasive cosmetic procedure that involves the manual exfoliation of the epidermis (the outermost layer of your skin) with a sterile surgical blade. As a result, all the dead skin cells and vellus hair (peach fuzz) are removed, leaving your skin smooth and glowy. This procedure is great for those looking to soften the appearance of fine lines and wrinkles and even out the skin’s surface. Due to its smoothing abilities, dermaplaning is an incredible pre-event treatment.

Resurfacer

$40

For a deeper more comprehensive form of exfoliation, you can incorporate any of our DermaQuest resurfacing treatments (Lactic Acid, Pumpkin, Mangobrite & C Infusion) into your facial.

LED Light Therapy

$55

This add-on LED Light Therapy utilizes therapeutic wavelengths of light to enhance collagen and elastin production (red light - 630nm), combat the overproduction of acne-causing sebaceous oils, and kill off p.acneus bacteria (blue light - 415nm), reduce redness, and improve overall circulation. Infrared light (830 nm) accelerates the healing process by promoting lymphatic flow. The mode will be selected by the Esthetician based on consultation and the desired course of treatment.
