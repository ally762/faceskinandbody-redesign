Try Our Purifying Acne Facial for Congested, Acneic, and Oily Skin!

At Face Skin & Body Bar in Calgary, we understand the unique challenges of struggling with breakouts, excess oil, and persistent congestion. Our specialized acne facial is expertly formulated as a deep cleanse and purification skin treatment, designed to address these specific concerns. If you're seeking to restore your skin's natural balance, gently exfoliate, and draw out impurities, our purifying acne facial offers a targeted path to a radiant and refreshed complexion.

What Is A Purifying Acne Facial?

Our Purifying Acne Facial is more than just a standard skin treatment; it's a comprehensive approach to combating the root causes of breakouts and congestion. This specialized acne-clearing facial is designed to provide a deep cleanse and purification for skin prone to breakouts, excess oil, and visible pores.

The treatment begins with a thorough cleansing, followed by targeted exfoliation to gently lift away dead skin cells and prepare the skin for deeper work. Key to this acne facial is the careful process of extractions, where our skilled estheticians unclog pores, removing blackheads, whiteheads, and other impurities that contribute to congestion and breakouts. This step is crucial for clearing existing blemishes and preventing new ones from forming, significantly improving the overall texture of the skin.

Our extended 75-minute Purifying Acne Facial also includes a clarifying Hydrojelly Mask. This professional-grade mask works synergistically with the facial to soothe inflammation, calm irritated skin, and further draw out impurities from deep within the pores. To further enhance results, a session of Blue Light Therapy is incorporated. Blue Light Therapy is scientifically proven to target and eliminate acne-causing bacteria on the skin's surface, reducing inflammation and accelerating the healing process of active breakouts. This multi-step approach ensures a purifying and restorative acne-clearing facial, leaving your skin less congested and more balanced.

Benefits Of Purifying Acne Facials

Embracing our Purifying Acne Facial offers a number of benefits for anyone struggling with breakout-prone skin:

  • Deep Cleansing and Detoxification
  • Reduced Breakouts and Impurities
  • Minimized Pore Size Appearance
  • Balanced Oil Production
  • Reduced Inflammation and Redness
  • Improved Skin Texture and Tone
  • Enhanced Skin Health
  • Professional Expertise

Acne facials at Face Skin & Body Bar in Calgary are designed to clear congested skin, reduce active breakouts, and prevent future acne by cleansing pores, removing oil and debris, and performing safe extractions. These treatments minimize the appearance of enlarged pores, balance oil production, and reduce redness and inflammation through targeted therapies, such as Blue Light and Hydrojelly masks. The result is smoother texture, more even skin tone, and healthier skin overall, delivered by trained professionals for long-term improvement. However, this is not achieved through one treatment alone. The best course of action is to incorporate medical grade products along with regular facials and / or medical grade peels to really make a difference to acne prone skin.

Ideal Candidates

  • Congested pores and frequent blackheads
  • Mild to moderate acne breakouts, including papules and pustules
  • Oily skin that often appears shiny or feels greasy
  • Uneven skin texture due to breakouts or clogged pores
  • Dull or devitalized skin that needs deep purification and exfoliation
  • Inflammation and redness associated with active blemishes

During your initial visit or at your first consultation, our experienced estheticians will thoroughly assess your skin type and specific skin concerns to confirm if this acne-clearing facial is the most suitable and effective solution for achieving your desired clear and balanced complexion.

What To Expect From Treatment

Your 60 or 75-minute Purifying Acne Facial at Face Skin & Body Bar is a comprehensive and calming experience, specially designed to address your skin concerns. The process typically includes:

01

Deep Cleanse

Your skin will be thoroughly cleansed to remove makeup, surface impurities, and excess oil, preparing it for the treatment.

02

Gentle Exfoliation

A targeted exfoliation step will gently slough away dead skin cells and prepare the pores for deeper purification.

03

Steam and Preparatory Masque

Steam will be used to soften the skin and open pores, followed by a preparatory masque to draw out impurities and make extractions more efficient.

04

Expert Extractions

This crucial step involves meticulous and gentle extractions performed by our skilled estheticians to unclog pores, remove blackheads, whiteheads, and other forms of congestion, reducing inflammation and improving skin texture.

05

Clarifying Hydrojelly Mask (75 min only)

A specialized Hydrojelly Mask designed to clarify and soothe will be applied. This mask delivers potent ingredients deep into the skin, helping to calm inflammation and further purify pores.

06

Blue Light Therapy (75 min only)

A 15-minute session of therapeutic Blue Light Therapy targets acne-causing bacteria, reduces inflammation, and promotes healing, further enhancing the acne-clearing facial results.

07

Finishing Products

The treatment concludes with the application of professional-grade serums, moisturizers, and SPF tailored to your skin's needs, leaving your skin balanced and protected.

Number Of Treatments And Frequency Required

While a single Purifying Acne Facial may provide some immediate improvement, for optimal and sustained acne clearing, a series of treatments or having them in concert with Medical-Grade Products and / or Medical Grade Peels are typically recommended. The exact number and frequency will depend on the severity of your acne and congestion, and your skin's response to the treatment. Generally, clients benefit from monthly treatments, 4 to 6 weeks apart, followed by maintenance facials as needed. Your esthetician will create a personalized treatment plan and recommend any products during your initial visit, as requested.

Treatment Areas

Our Purifying Acne Facial is primarily focused on the face, as this is the most common area for acne, congestion, and enlarged pores. However, the principles and techniques can be adapted to treat other areas prone to breakouts, such as the:

  • Neck
  • Chest (Décolleté)
  • Back

Please discuss any body acne concerns during your consultation.

Results And Recovery

After your Purifying Acne Facial, your skin will feel wonderfully cleansed and refreshed. You might notice a little redness or tenderness, especially if you had extensive extractions. Don't worry—any temporary redness from extractions usually calms down within just a few hours.

Over the days that follow, it's completely normal to notice some initial "purging"—a sign that deeper impurities are actively moving to the skin's surface. As your skin continues to balance and heal, you may observe a reduction in blackheads, a minimized appearance of clogged pores, and a calming of overall inflammation. With a recommended series of treatments, you'll experience long-term improvement in acne management, leading to clearer, healthier, and more balanced skin.
