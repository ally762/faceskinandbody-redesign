Face Skin & Body Bar Unveils The Skin Investment Club With Launch Event

November 17, 2025Written by Heather Dybvig

CALGARY, AB: Face Skin & Body Bar, a medical spa in Calgary, is proud to announce the launch of its Skin Investment Club with the Skin Investment Club - Unveiled Event, taking place on Wednesday, November 5th, from 4:30 to 7:30. The event will feature stations geared towards acne, anti-aging and discolouration, complimentary consultations, food, wine, and once-a-year savings on some of their most popular advanced treatment series.

The Skin Investment Club features three tiers: The Smart Skin Plan, The Smart Skin Plan Plus, and the Accelerated Smart Skin Plan. Each of these tiers offers clients a Beauty Bank: 100% of the plan payment goes into the bank to help clients save for services to help them on their skin care journey. The plans offer a 10%, 12%, or 15% discount on products and services, depending on the plan. Additionally, plan members can get a $25, $50, or $100 referral credit, depending on how much their referral spends in a visit.

“The Skin Investment Club - Unveiled Event is going to be so much fun, and we encourage anyone with an interest in skincare to attend,” said Heather Dybvig, the co-founder and Manager of Operations at Face Skin & Body Bar. “We’re going to feature demonstrations of some of the products and services we offer, great food, great wine, discounts on our memberships, and a lot of incredible people who want to help you look and feel your best.”

Face Skin & Body Bar offers a variety of products and services, including chemical peels, microneedling, facials, injectables, fillers, laser hair removal, and medical-grade skincare products. The team helps clients determine which treatments are right for their skin, body, and goals, then creates a comprehensive treatment plan. It also offers specialized treatment plans like the Exclusive Lift & Define Collection, the Bridal Skin Prep Collection, and the Texture & Tone Transformation. These plans offer skincare over the course of several weeks or months to achieve your desired results.
