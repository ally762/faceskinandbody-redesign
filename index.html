Fine Lines & Aging

As we age, our skin undergoes significant structural and functional changes which leave us with reduced levels of collagen, elastin, and oil production. These changes, coupled with other age-related factors such as genetics, sun exposure, lifestyle choices, and hormonal shifts, can lead to fine lines and wrinkles, as well as noticeable volume loss over time. Hyperpigmentation/Sun Damage

Hyperpigmentation is a common skin condition that occurs when certain patches of the skin appear visibly darker than the surrounding areas. These hyperpigmented areas are caused by an excess of melanin, a pigment-producing substance in human skin, eyes, and hair.

Common examples of hyperpigmentation include sun damage, acne marks, freckles, age spots, and melasma. Achieving an even skin tone can be challenging, but understanding hyperpigmentation is a crucial first step towards achieving clear, vibrant skin.

To help address this concern, we recommend the following list of treatments and products, which might be right for you.

Dry/Dehydrated

In our dry Calgary climate, dehydrated skin is a concern that we are all familiar with. Common signs are dull, tight and/or cracking skin, along with possible discomfort like sensitivity or redness. In some cases, dry skin might be associated with other skin concerns such as eczema, psoriasis and dermatitis. A combination of internal and external causes can perpetuate the severity, including incorrect product use, climate and diet. Acne

Acne-prone skin is characterized by both non-inflammatory breakouts and inflammatory red, tender papules or cystic lesions. While this condition is more prevalent in individuals with oily skin, it can affect any skin type. Common triggers include incorrect product usage, hormones, diet, medication, genetics, and environmental factors. Appropriate management of acne requires addressing these factors in a comprehensive manner. To help target this skin concern, the following treatments and products might be right for you.

Sensitive/Rosacea

Skin sensitivity is a common condition that is presented with burning, itching, and/or tight, red skin. Individuals with sensitive skin can have greater reactions to a variety of services and products. Common reasons for skin sensitivity are damage to the skin barrier, medications, environmental causes, rosacea, and more.
