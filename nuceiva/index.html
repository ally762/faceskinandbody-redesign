Why does Everyone Says "Botox"

In the realm of aesthetic treatments, the name BOTOX® has become a generic label for all injectables that smooth wrinkles. This is much like using Kleenex™ for any facial tissue, regardless of the actual brand.

However, BOTOX® is just one of several neuromodulator medications officially approved by Health Canada. Each prescription has unique properties that affect things like how quickly it works and how far it spreads. Understanding these differences is key to achieving optimal natural results.

During your comprehensive initial visit, our Registered Nurse Injector will assess your medical history and specific goals. After carefully examining the movement and depth of the lines and wrinkles you wish to treat, they will partner with our Medical Director to prescribe the specific neuromodulator (which may be Nuceiva™) best suited for your unique skin and anatomy.

How Nuceiva™ Works

Nuceiva™ is an injectable prescription medicine that acts directly on the underlying facial muscles. Years of squinting, frowning, and laughing cause these facial muscles to repeatedly contract, eventually creating static fine lines and wrinkles in the skin.

This treatment works by temporarily blocking the nerve signal that commands the muscle contractions. When the muscle is relaxed, the overlying skin smooths out. This action both diminishes existing deep lines and halts the development of new ones, promoting a lasting youthful appearance. The Nuceiva™ procedure is fast, targeting the precise root cause of these expression-based wrinkles.

Benefits Of Nuceiva™

Integrating Nuceiva™ into your aesthetic treatment plan offers significant benefits for your skin:

  • Softens Expression Lines: Dramatically reduces the visibility of moderate-to-severe frown lines, crow's feet, and forehead wrinkles.
  • A Natural Rejuvenation: Our careful placement of Nuceiva™ injections ensures movement is preserved where appropriate, delivering beautifully subtle and natural results.
  • Convenient & Quick: The treatment takes very little time, allowing for a quick return to your daily schedule with minimal downtime.
  • Proactive Aging: Regular use of Nuceiva™ is an essential component of a proactive treatment plan to prevent the formation of deeper, static lines.

Integrated Treatment: Nuceiva™ works seamlessly with other skincare treatments to achieve comprehensive facial rejuvenation.

Ideal Candidates

If you are troubled by dynamic lines that appear or deepen when you express yourself, Nuceiva™ is likely an excellent treatment choice. It is ideal for those seeking to soften fine lines and wrinkles without looking "frozen."

Nuceiva™ is highly effective for addressing:

  • The vertical frown lines (glabella) between the eyebrows
  • The horizontal lines across the forehead
  • Crow's feet at the corners of the eyes
  • Contouring the jawline with masseter injections

During your consultation, we will confirm if Nuceiva™ is the perfect product to achieve your desired youthful appearance.

What To Expect From Treatment

Your Nuceiva™ treatment begins with a highly personalized consultation to review your facial expressions and confirm your goal for natural results. The actual treatment involves quick, precise injections into the target muscle groups.

Number Of Treatments Required

A single treatment of Nuceiva™ typically provides a duration of three to four months of smooth skin. To maintain your refined look, we generally recommend repeating the Nuceiva™ injections 3 to 4 times annually as part of your overall treatment plan.

Treatment Cost

Our Nuceiva™ treatment plan primarily targets the upper face, including the forehead and crow's feet. We also utilize Nuceiva™ for therapeutic uses, such as reducing the size of the masseter muscles to relieve teeth grinding or slim the jawline.

Results And Recovery

Following your Nuceiva™ injections, you will experience minimal downtime. Any minor redness or swelling at the injection sites usually resolves within a few hours.

While you may start to see some relaxation of lines after a few days, the full natural and youthful results of your Nuceiva™ treatment are typically visible after about two weeks. We encourage a complimentary follow-up at the two-week mark to review your progress and fine-tune your long-term treatment plan.
