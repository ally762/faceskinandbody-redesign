Evoke / Define: Non-Invasive Lower Facelift in Calgary

Book Consultation+1 (403) 454-5607
Overview

The desire for a more youthful appearance is universal, but the prospect of surgery can be daunting. The lower face—prone to sagging skin, jowls, and the stubborn "double chin"—is often the first area to reveal signs of aging.

Today, many individuals are also facing a new aesthetic challenge: "GLP-1 face." Rapid weight loss from medications like OzempicTM or WegovyTM can lead to significant facial volume loss, leaving behind loose, hanging skin and a prematurely aged appearance.

We are proud to offer Evoke / Define, a revolutionary, hands-free radiofrequency (RF) treatment that delivers highly effective, non-invasive lower face contouring with zero downtime. This cutting-edge procedure remodels your face and neck, specifically targeting skin tightening and the restoration of your natural contours.

What is a Radiofrequency Non-Invasive Lower Facelift?

An Evoke / Define non-invasive lower facelift is an advanced aesthetic treatment that uses clinically proven Bipolar Radiofrequency (RF) technology to remodel the facial tissues. Unlike traditional surgery, this procedure is pain-free and requires no incisions or recovery time. It is a highly customizable treatment designed to:

  • Tighten skin following rapid weight loss (GLP-1 medications).
  • Define the jawline and lift sagging jowls.
  • Reduce stubborn fat in the submental area (double chin).

The Challenge of the Lower Face

The lower face—encompassing the cheeks, jawline, and the area under the chin—is often the first place to betray signs of aging or weight fluctuations.

  • The "Weight Loss Gap": While GLP-1 medications can be an effective tool for weight management, the skin’s elasticity often struggles to keep pace. As the fat beneath the surface disappears quickly, it can leave the face looking 'deflated' or the jawline sagging.
  • Gravity's Toll: Skin naturally loses elasticity over time, leading to the formation of jowls that obscure a once-defined profile.
  • The Tightening Gap: Many fat reduction methods eliminate fat but fail to address the resulting loose skin. Evoke / Define solves this by tightening the "envelope" of the skin as it contours the face.
  • Stubborn Fat: The submental area (double chin) is notoriously difficult to address through diet and exercise alone, even for the slimmest individuals.

How do Non-Invasive Lower Facelifts Work ?

The success of the Evoke / Define treatment lies in its targeted, hands-free application of RF energy.

  1. Cheek & Jawline Applicators: These comfortably deliver thermal energy deep into the subdermal layers, initiating collagen production and remodeling. This is essential for patients experiencing volume loss, as it helps "shrink-wrap" the skin to the new facial structure, lifting the cheeks and softening nasolabial folds.
  2. Chin Applicator: This specifically targets the subcutaneous fat deposits that form a double chin. The controlled temperature causes apoptosis (fat cell destruction) while simultaneously tightening the overlying skin. This is key, as other fat reduction treatments often fail to address the resulting loose skin. Evoke / Define provides a comprehensive treatment that destroys fat and ensures maximum skin tightening for a more defined profile.

The Experience: The procedure is completely painless—you simply feel a gentle, sustained warmth.

Benefits of a Non-Invasive Lower Facelift

The Non-Invasive Lower Facelift treatment offers incredible benefits for those seeking facial improvement without the risks or recovery of surgery:

  • Defined Jawline & Contours: Effectively reduces jowls and firms the skin for a sharper, more youthful appearance.
  • Double Chin Reduction: Destroys stubborn submental fat while providing crucial skin tightening.
  • Enhanced Collagen: Stimulates new collagen production, leading to firmer, more resilient skin.
  • Comfort & Convenience: The treatment is painless, non-invasive, and requires 0 days of downtime.
  • Treats/Benefits: Sagging skin, jowls, undefined jawline, double chin, and nasolabial folds

The Difference: Evoke vs XERF™

While both treatments utilize radiofrequency (RF) energy to rejuvenate the face, they operate on very different levels. XERF™ is a manual tool for surface tightening, whereas we utilize Evoke for its superior ability to provide structural remodeling and facial contouring.

Why our clinic chose evoke

We’ve invested in Evoke because we believe our patients deserve the most consistent and structural results possible. Here is why Evoke is our gold standard:

  • Architectural Contouring: XERF™ is primarily a "skin" treatment. Evoke goes deeper, remodeling the subdermal layers and the fibroseptal network. It doesn't just tighten the surface; it reshapes the foundation to sharpen your jawline and reduce jowl heaviness.
  • Precision via Automated: Manual devices like XERF™ and our Forma RF device rely on the technician's movement. Evoke uses built in automation to monitor skin temperature 1,000 times per second. This ensures the entire area stays at the optimal temperature for collagen production without the "spicy" hot spots common in manual treatments.
  • The Effortless Experience: Evoke is the ultimate "lunchtime" treatment. You can relax, catch up on emails, or read while the technology performs a consistent, medical-grade lift.

  • Sagging skin or jowls along the jawline.
  • The appearance of a double chin or submental fullness.
  • Those seeking skin tightening and lifting without a surgical procedure.
  • Patients looking for a comfortable, non-invasive treatment with zero downtime.

What To Expect From The Treatment

The Evoke / Define procedure is designed for ultimate comfort and convenience, allowing you to relax, read, or even work during your session.

Number Of Treatments Required

Your precise treatment plan will be determined during your consultation, based on your goals and the skin's response, but the required commitment is typically:

  • Skin Tigething: 4 to 6 sessions, performed once a week.
  • Subcutaneous Fat (Double Chin): 6 to 8 sessions, performed once a week.

Potential Results

Initial improvement is often visible around 3 weeks after the last treatment.

  • Results continue to improve over the next 3 months as new collagen and elastin are generated.

The final result is a gradual, more natural lifting and enhanced definition.

The Cost of AN Evoke / Define Treatment(s)

The cost of your Evoke / Define treatment plan will be discussed during your consultation and is based on the number of sessions required to achieve your natural improvement. It is a significantly more affordable treatment option compared to a lower face lift surgery.
